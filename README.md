# Kubb alpha

Test builds of Kubb by Summer Grove Games: the Viking lawn game, played in a lodge hall.

Download the APK from **Releases** (on the right), install it on your Android phone, and allow "install unknown apps" once. New test builds install over the old one.

Play online against a friend, play the computer (Easy to Expert), or pass one phone back and forth.

## Latest: build 6 (0.1.0-6)

**Three ways to play.** Pick one on the title screen. They differ in what happens to a field kubb once it's knocked over.
- **Mortal:** 6 batons every turn. A field kubb knocked over is gone for good. The quickest game.
- **Graveyard Resurrection** (the default): 6 batons every turn. A field kubb knocked over stays lying where it fell. If a kubb you toss touches one lying in the far half, it stands back up.
- **Championship:** the official rules. 2 batons on the first turn, 4 on the second, then 6. Every kubb knocked down is thrown back. The longest game.

**Set up your back row.** In Mortal and Graveyard, place your 5 kubbs anywhere along your line before the first throw.

**Your kubbs.** After you choose how to play, pick a colour, a style and a wood, with a 3D preview. Everything past plain birch is a premium look that's free to try for now.
- **Styles:** Wood, Runes, Fire, Lightning, Acid.
- **Woods:** Birch, Black & White Ebony, Purpleheart, Padauk, Zebrawood.

**Also new**
- An "Advantage!" banner and a glowing gold line when a player moves up.
- A 3D coin for the coin toss.
- A new hearth fire and rune title.
- How to play shows the rules for the mode you picked.
- Field kubbs keep their owner's colour.
- Knocking the king over with a tossed kubb loses the game.

**Good to know**
- Online play needs both players on build 6. Earlier builds can't join.
- Online play hasn't been tested on two phones yet. If something goes wrong, tell us what you saw.
